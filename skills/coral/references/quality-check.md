# Output checks

## Truth and consistency

- Identical experience facts in each requested format. STAR does not claim a different outcome than CORAL.
- No inflation of responsibility: "I" describes actual ownership, not the team's entire work.
- Correct baseline, numerator, denominator, units, dates/time span, and measurement/estimate status.
- Clearly differentiate a delivered outcome from a goal, prediction, correlation, or estimate.
- If a user corrects a fact, update all affected outputs. Never silently retain the outdated version.
- Ask about a contradiction rather than smooth it away.

## STAR

- One readable bullet by default; specific contribution and tangible result.
- No unnecessary scene-setting, vague adjectives, or unsupported metrics.
- Situation, task, action, and result are covered where known; no need to display the labels.

## CORAL

- Brief context, clear objective and precise personal ownership.
- Give actual outcome before the detailed actions; then explain **the real reasons** behind decisions rather than inventing them after the fact.
- Credible execution steps; honest obstacles; concrete learning if supplied.
- Natural spoken answer, not a memorized list of five labels.
- Can withstand "What did *you* do?", "Why?", "How do you know?", "What would you change?".

## Privacy

- Never demand a name, phone number, email address, exact employer, or proprietary documents.
- Avoid echoing unnecessary personal identifiers back into outputs.
- Do not save material to disk or send to third parties without explicit permission; hosted model processing is governed by the chosen service.
